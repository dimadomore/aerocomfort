# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

English-speaking expats and international homeowners (plus local Spanish residents) on the Costa Blanca and Costa Cálida — Torrevieja, Orihuela Costa and 45+ surrounding towns. Predominantly 40+, many retirees or second-home owners. They are hot, frequently non-Spanish-speaking, and wary of being overcharged or ghosted by a tradesperson. They arrive on a phone, in summer, wanting one thing: a trustworthy local air-conditioning specialist who speaks their language, quotes clearly, and shows up. Job to be done: *"find someone I can trust to install / repair / service my AC — fast, without language friction or pricing games — and reach them in one tap."*

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for AeroComfort, an HVAC business (air conditioning, heat pumps / aerotermia, ventilation) run by an autónomo (Gregorio) on the Spanish costas. It converts search and referral traffic into leads — a WhatsApp message, a phone call, or a quote-form submission — by establishing trust faster than a Tilda template or a competitor can. Fully bilingual EN/ES, every page mirrored. Success = the visitor contacts AeroComfort instead of bouncing, already believing "these people are legit, local, and easy to deal with."

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, fresh, expert. The voice is a calm, competent local specialist — plain-spoken, reassuring, never salesy or hyped. Warmth comes from being genuinely helpful and clear (clear pricing, tidy work, real guarantees); energy comes from the product itself: cool, fresh, conditioned air on a hot coast. Emotional goal: relief and confidence — *"finally, someone who'll just sort it out and not rip me off."* Comfortable serving an international, multilingual clientele.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-template landing pages: repeated uppercase tracked eyebrows over every section, identical icon-tile card grids, flat single-stroke icons, hollow "trust" badges with no substance behind them.
- Cheap tradesperson / clip-art HVAC sites: stock-photo overload, clashing red-and-yellow, "CALL NOW!!" urgency, comic-sans energy.
- Over-designed SaaS / fintech aesthetics: dark mode, purple→blue gradients, glassmorphism everywhere, the hero-metric template. Wrong register for a hands-on local service.
- Anything colder or more corporate than a trusted local specialist; anything that adds friction or doubt for a non-technical 40+ reader.

## Design Principles

- **Earn trust in the details.** A 40+ homeowner judges competence by craft. Every icon, card, border and number must feel considered and real — sloppy details read as "will they be this sloppy on my install too?"
- **Sell the feeling of cool air.** The product is relief from heat. Lean into fresh, airy, cool-blue depth and subtle air / temperature / breeze motifs so the site *feels* like what it sells — without gimmicks.
- **One tap, no language wall.** Contact (WhatsApp / phone / quote) is always within reach and obvious; copy stays plain EN/ES, never jargon. Reduce load for a hot, hurried, often non-native reader.
- **Calm confidence over hype.** Reassure, don't shout. Clarity, generous space, and honest specifics beat exclamation marks and fake urgency.
- **Show, don't claim.** Back "trusted / certified / guaranteed" with concrete, verifiable substance (5.0 Google rating, named brands, real cert facts, the town list) — not empty badges.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Primary audience is 40+, often reading on a phone in bright sunlight: 18px base body, ≥4.5:1 body-text contrast (≥3:1 large text), tap targets ≥44px, always-visible focus rings. Bilingual EN/ES with correct hreflang. Reduced-motion honored on every animation (already implemented). Never rely on color alone for meaning; keep body line length 65–75ch.
